Interhouse Sports is a one-day community event founded in 2023 where competition meets culture. Rooted in friendly rivalry and team spirit, it brings together people from all walks of life to compete in track, field, and street-style games backed by live DJs, food, and nonstop vibes. Whether you're here to run, cheer, or just catch the energy, there's a house for you.
